-------------------- 동아리 소개 --------------------

[소개 작은 제목]
ABOUT THE CLUB

[소개 메인 제목]
TC has built its tradition and know-how since 2012.

[소개 설명]
TC aims to be more than just a high school club. Students take ownership of their roles, work as a team, and grow into future interdisciplinary problem-solvers. Years of experience have led to strong competition results, including first-place finishes. TC has also been selected for the KLA scholarship program for multiple consecutive years, expanding both academic and research opportunities.

[소개 카드 1 제목]
Mechanical Design · Build

[소개 카드 1 설명]
Each season, we adjust the frame to meet the rules, compare possible approaches, and build the robot based on what works most efficiently.

[소개 카드 2 제목]
Robot Programming

[소개 카드 2 설명]
We control motors and sensors and write autonomous and driver-control code.

[소개 카드 3 제목]
Repeated Testing

[소개 카드 3 설명]
We recreate real match situations, find problems, and continuously improve both the robot structure and the code.

[소개 카드 4 제목]
Solve It as a Team

[소개 카드 4 설명]
Mechanical, programming, and strategy roles connect with one another to complete a single robot.

[신입부원 작은 제목]
FOR NEW MEMBERS

[신입부원 제목]
Not the student who already knows everything,
but the student who wants to try.

[신입부원 설명]
It is okay if robotics or coding is completely new to you. If you are interested in building, fixing, and trying again, you can learn through TC activities.

-------------------- 활동 · 대회 --------------------

[활동 작은 제목]
ACTIVITIES · COMPETITIONS

[활동 메인 제목]
Build it. Code it.
Prove it in competition.

[활동 설명]
When an actual competition and season schedule are confirmed, this page can be updated simply by changing the text. The flow below shows the typical process TC follows while preparing for robotics competitions such as FTC.

[1단계 제목]
Game Analysis

[1단계 설명]
Review the rules and challenges, then decide which robot functions and match strategies are needed.

[2단계 제목]
Design

[2단계 설명]
Compare ideas and plan the robot structure and mechanisms.

[3단계 제목]
Build

[3단계 설명]
Assemble the frame and components to complete the physical robot.

[4단계 제목]
Programming

[4단계 설명]
Implement sensors, motors, autonomous routines, and driver-control code.

[5단계 제목]
Testing · Competition

[5단계 설명]
Repeat testing and improvement, then operate the robot in actual matches.
